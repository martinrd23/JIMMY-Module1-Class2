Negative forms

6

Use aren’t / are not to make simple negative statements.

Full form

We are not late.
You are not from Brazil.
They are not supervisors.

Short form

We aren’t late.
You aren’t from Brazil.
They aren’t supervisors.

Questions

How questions

14

Use How old and How + information to ask for details.

How old…?

How old are you?
I’m 32.

How…?

How are you?
I’m good, thanks.

How often appears in useful fixed questions. Learn the whole chunk.

Questions

Short answers

15

Answer naturally without repeating the whole question every time.

QuestionShort answer
Are you Peruvian?Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
Are they engineers?Yes, they are. / No, they aren’t.
Are you in operations?Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
Use short answers to sound natural and keep the conversation moving.
